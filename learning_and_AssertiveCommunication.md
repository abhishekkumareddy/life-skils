## Question 1

**What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?**

1. Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts. Focus on the speaker and topic instead.
1. Try not to interrupt the other person. Let them finish and then respond.
2. Use door openers. These are phrases that show your are interested and keep the other person talking.
3. Show that you are listening with body language.
4. If appropriate take notes during important conversations.
5. Paraphrase what other have said to make sure you are on the same page



## Question 2
 
**According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?**

Reflective Listening is a communication strategy in which a listener improves their understanding of the speaker's idea by offering that understanding back to the speaker in order to confirm that the idea has been understood correctly
