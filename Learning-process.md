# Learning Process

## Question 1

**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line?**

The Feynman Technique is a four-step mental framework for understanding any concept:

1. Choose a concept.
2. Teach it to a child.
3. Identify the gaps.
4. Simplify and use analogies.

## Question 3

**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

- **Active mode:** Tight, structured thinking used for familiar patterns.
- **Diffused mode:** A relaxed, resting state that lets your mind wander to grasp new concepts.
