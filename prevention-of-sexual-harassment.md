# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?
* **Sexual harassment means any unwanted sexual behavior at work that makes you feel unsafe or uncomfortable.**
* This includes bad jokes, rude comments, showing dirty pictures, or unwanted touching.
* It also includes bosses asking for sexual favors in exchange for promotions or better pay.
* How the behavior makes the victim feel is what matters, not what the person meant to do.

## 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?
* **Tell the person clearly to stop and report them to HR or your company's complaints team right away.**
* Say "no" firmly so they know their behavior is not okay.
* Write down the dates, times, places, what happened, and who else saw it.
* If the problem does not stop, or if it is very serious, call the police for help.
